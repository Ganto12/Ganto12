# Mental models: general problem solving across domains, in pursuit of mastery

General mental models and insights for problem solving across many domains (games, sport, markets, startups and GPUs), always in pursuit of mastery.

## The models

- **Measure first, then iterate:** pick the stat that decides the game, measure where I stand, set an expected value, compare it with what happened, and go again.
- **Find the broken slot machine:** every system has an edge somewhere (a game meta, a football tactic, a market anomaly, a platform gap). Search constantly, exploit fast, size the bet, and move on when it closes, because every edge decays: explore/exploit, played at speed.
- **Find the 80/20 everywhere:** the few moves that win most rounds, matches or benchmarks.
- **Road to mastery, always the student:** a beginner's openness with senior-level self-review, auditing my own approach against the current state of the art.
- **Discipline as a by-product of chasing mastery:** 10,000 hours, or 10,000 iterations. Start from the greats; the deeper you go, the more your own style emerges.
- **High agency:** if something is broken and nobody owns it, take it, without waiting for a course, permission or a manager.
- **Burn the boats:** when something really matters, remove the way back so the only path is forward.
- **Startup speed plus research depth:** speed finds the edge before it closes; research rigour finds the edges others miss and proves they are real.
- **The brain as the inverse of NVLink scaling:** add GPUs and each is worth a little less, because the links become the bottleneck; add domains to a dense brain and each is worth more, because the links are already there.
- **The world as a hyperfast market:** small reversible bets, same-day feedback.
- **Test hard, follow the evidence (Jim Simons):** you don't need every inner working of the machine to make it cook; directional bets that are right beat microscopic precision.
- **Problem-solving vigilance over efficiency vigilance:** stay on the real problem instead of optimising the wrong thing.
- **Factories over one-offs:** unambiguous sub-modes, one canonical way to run each, no back doors, a dedicated skill per factory.
