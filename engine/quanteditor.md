# QuantEditor: Markovian and goal-directed video editing, learned from top creators

Learns how top creators cut and writes the cut plan for a new script. Editing is modelled as part Markovian (each shot length depends on the last) and part teleological (a section-level cut-rate goal steers the whole path), decoded with dynamic programming.
